Artificial intelligence

  • AI-01 Classical Artificial Intelligence The AI that decides rather than predicts, in the scope Russell and Norvig gave it: rational agents and environment types, uninformed search, heuristics and A*, local search, constraint satisfaction and arc consistency, SAT and conflict-driven clause learning, minimax and Monte Carlo tree search, propositional and first-order logic, knowledge representation and description logics, STRIPS and PDDL planning with derived heuristics, planning under time and uncertainty, the frame problem, Bayesian networks and d-separation, HMMs and Kalman and particle filters, utility theory and value of information, MDPs and POMDPs, tabular reinforcement learning and the deadly triad, game theory and mechanism design, motion planning and SLAM, the record of what symbolic AI shipped, and the evidence on whether language models can plan. 22 modules · 125 resources · 2026-08-09
  • AI-02 Classical Machine Learning Statistical machine learning for structured and tabular data: empirical risk minimisation and the i.i.d. assumption, the bias–variance decomposition, ridge and lasso through the SVD, logistic regression and probability calibration, convex optimisation and duality, cross-validation and the four kinds of data leakage, kernels and support vector machines, decision trees, bagging and random forests, gradient boosting with XGBoost, LightGBM and CatBoost, k-means and Gaussian mixtures with EM, PCA and the lies t-SNE and UMAP tell, Bayesian inference, MCMC and Gaussian processes, PAC learning and VC dimension, feature engineering and missing data, time-series backtesting, causal inference and confounding, SHAP and interpretability, distribution shift and fairness, and double descent and TabPFN. 20 modules · 91 resources · 2026-08-09
  • AI-03 Deep Learning Neural networks one layer below the framework API: backpropagation as reverse-mode automatic differentiation, SGD and Adam and AdamW, initialisation and batch and layer normalisation, residual connections, the generalisation puzzle and random labels and grokking, convolutional networks from LeNet to ConvNeXt, LSTMs and the attention bottleneck, the transformer with multi-head attention and RoPE, FlashAttention and state-space models and mixture of experts, distributed training with ZeRO and pipeline parallelism and the memory arithmetic, Chinchilla scaling laws, contrastive and masked self-supervised learning, vision transformers and CLIP, graph neural networks and the Weisfeiler-Lehman bound, DQN and PPO, neural tangent kernels and lottery tickets, superposition and induction heads and sparse autoencoders, adversarial examples and calibration, and how to debug a training run. 21 modules · 99 resources · 2026-08-09
  • AI-04 Generative AI: Large Language Models The full LLM pipeline: next-token prediction and perplexity, byte-pair-encoding tokenisation and the arithmetic failures it causes, RoPE and RMSNorm and SwiGLU and grouped-query attention, pretraining data filtering and deduplication and contamination, Chinchilla budgeting and why production models are overtrained, distributed pretraining and loss spikes, evaluation and benchmark rot and LLM-as-judge bias, supervised fine-tuning and LoRA and QLoRA, preference optimisation from RLHF through DPO and SimPO to GRPO, reasoning with chain-of-thought and test-time compute and RLVR, KV caches and paged attention and speculative decoding and quantisation, long context and lost-in-the-middle, in-context learning, retrieval-augmented generation, tool use and agents, constrained decoding, interpretability, jailbreaks and prompt injection, hallucination and semantic entropy, distillation, and serving economics. 22 modules · 107 resources · 2026-08-09
